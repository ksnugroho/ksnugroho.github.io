I am an AI researcher and educator with 6+ years of experience in applied machine learning and AI-driven systems, spanning end-to-end pipelines from model development to deployment. My work sits at the intersection of academic research, industry collaboration, and large-scale AI education.

I am an AI Research Associate at the Bioinformatics & Data Science Research Center, Bina Nusantara University, where I design and maintain the research infrastructure at the NVIDIA–BINUS AI Research & Development Center (AIRDC) and conduct applied AI research with internal and external partners. I am also a Platinum-Tier Certified Instructor and University Ambassador for the NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute. I hold a Master of Computer Science in Intelligent Systems from Brawijaya University and a Professional Engineer (Ir.) degree in Informatics Engineering from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS).
